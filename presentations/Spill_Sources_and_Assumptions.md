## Assumptions and limitations

- The supplied brief and repository establish a local frontend prototype, not a deployed university system.
- No university endorsement, real usage, pilot outcome, cost saving, AI accuracy, or compliance certification is claimed.
- Response tracking, more granular institutional roles and multi-university configuration are proposed extensions.
- The three-key mechanism means three-person approval, not demonstrated cryptographic key splitting.
- All use-case scenarios are fictional. Interface counts are fixture data.
- Slides 14–17 use supplied design-reference images rather than current deployment screenshots.
- Diagrams and tables are proposed architecture or operational models unless their status says otherwise.
- The pilot, its staffing, its duration, and its continuation criteria require local decisions.
- Public identity protection does not make the system untraceable.
- Research provides general context and does not validate Spill or establish causation.
- No application code was changed and no university data was collected.
