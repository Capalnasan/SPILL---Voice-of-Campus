# AI Moderation

## AI Responsibilities

- Check whether a post is related to university life.
- Check whether the post is clear and specific enough.
- Suggest rewrites for vague posts.
- Detect spam, toxicity, threats, and sensitive accusations.
- Assign a category.
- Help summarize posts for weekly and monthly reports.

## Example Decisions

- "My boyfriend cheated on me" -> rejected because it is not campus-related.
- "This class is terrible" -> needs rewrite because it is too vague.
- "During today's course, the teacher showed a knife and I felt unsafe" -> accepted or escalated for sensitive review.
