# Product Notes

## Purpose

Spill gives the UPT community a safer way to report real academic and campus issues while keeping feedback structured enough to help the university act on it.

## Main User Groups

- Students
- Teachers
- Teaching assistants
- Administrative staff
- Quality Committee members
- Administrative Bureau members

## Main Issue Categories

- Teaching quality
- Exams and grading
- Administrative problems
- Infrastructure issues
- Safety concerns
- Communication problems
